Plan & tax levers

These levers shape how tax-efficient the plan is — pension top-ups before you stop work, annual Bed-&-ISA moves, and the order pots are drawn in retirement.

Before retirement

On a £75k salary, roughly £24,730 sits in the 40% band — contributions up to that get 40% relief (annual allowance £60k). Salary sacrifice also saves 2% NI.
Funded from cash every April while cash remains — shelters growth from tax for life.
e.g. a global or S&P 500 index fund inside the ISA.

It uses 2025/26 UK allowances (personal allowance £12,570, basic-rate band to £50,270, ISA allowance £20,000, pension annual allowance £60,000) held flat, simplified UFPLS-style pension withdrawals (25% tax-free / 75% taxable), and assumes UK legal-tender gold and silver coins (Sovereigns, Britannias) remain CGT-exempt.     Setup & User Guide

    Tap a section to open it. Every input field also has its own tooltip — hover, or long-press on a phone.

    1 · Getting started
    The Cockpit is one self-contained file — it runs in any browser, needs no account and sends nothing anywhere. Everything you type autosaves on the device you’re using.
    • Computer: open the HTML file and just start — edits save automatically.
    • iPhone: host the app folder (GitHub Pages or Netlify), open the address in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen. It then works offline, full-screen, with its own icon.
    • The hosted copy ships blank by design; your figures go on via Load scenario (JSON) and live only on the phone.
    2 · Getting your data in
    • ⚙ Settings — names, dates of birth, retirement dates, salaries, state pensions, pensions in payment and other income; tick Plan as a couple to add a partner.
    • Portfolio — add each holding with its wrapper, owner, start month, value and growth.
    • Moving between devices: Save scenario (JSON) on one, Load scenario (JSON) on the other (AirDrop is ideal — it never touches the internet).
    3 · The income tuner
    Drag the slider to set your target monthly income after tax, in today’s money — the whole plan recalculates live.
    • Plan funded to age works in reverse too: click the age, type a target, and the Cockpit solves the income that gets you there.
    • Escalate with inflation keeps spending power level; today’s money shows all figures in current purchasing power.
    4 · Portfolio
    • Each wrapper (pension, ISA, taxable, EIS, VCT, gold, cash, Premium Bonds, crypto) is taxed by its own rules — the coloured dot shows which is which.
    • Start month keeps money out of the plan until it actually arrives (an inheritance, a maturing bond).
    • Switch between table and tile views; tiles open an editor with a notes field (links become clickable). Reorder with the arrows; filter by name, wrapper or owner.
    • For taxable investments, the estimate button works out annual growth from what you paid vs what it’s worth.
    5 · Plan & tax levers
    • One-off pension contribution before retirement, with 20% or 40% relief, paid from a cash pot you choose.
    • ISA funding moves cash into shelter each April (£20k per person).
    • Drawdown order: Tax-optimised fills each personal allowance with zero-tax pension withdrawals first, then spends tax-free pots before taxed ones.
    • One-off expenses (car, holidays, family…) are drawn tax-efficiently in their year and flagged in the projection.
    6 · Actions
    Tiles for the real-world steps. Click one for detail and suggestions; give it a due date and it turns amber as the date nears (alert windows are set in Settings).
    • Ticking an action done applies its portfolio effect automatically (e.g. moving cash) and archives it — restorable from the archive.
    • Calendar (.ics) adds reminders to your phone’s calendar at both alert points and on the day; the email button drafts a summary to your address.
    7 · Projection
    Year by year to your chosen horizon: income need, state pensions, Fixed inc £ (pensions in payment and other income), pension drawdown, tax paid and every pot’s balance. Icons mark one-off expenses. Download CSV exports the full table, including per-wrapper balances, for a spreadsheet.
    8 · Settings reference
    Grouped into collapsible areas: You, Spouse / partner, Display & app, Action alerts, Plan horizon.
    • Retirement dates drive the countdown, salary cut-off and drawdown start — each of you can have your own.
    • Pensions in payment and ranged other income (e.g. part-time work) are taxed and prorated by month.
    • The app version label appears in the menu bar — handy for telling copies apart.
    9 · Reports, backups and resets
    • Save / Load scenario (JSON) — your backup and transfer format. Save one after any big change.
    • Download app + data — a single HTML file with your figures embedded: the whole app and plan in one portable, private file.
    • Clear all personal data wipes figures (it prompts you to save first); Reset to defaults restores the built-in example plan.
    10 · Troubleshooting
    • Old version on the phone — the offline cache is serving the previous copy: open the address in Safari while online, refresh, reopen the icon.
    • Data gone after clearing Safari data — that wipes home-screen app storage too; load your latest scenario JSON.
    • Live UK data blank — it needs an internet connection; the rest of the app works offline.
    • “Add to Home Screen” missing — you’re not in Safari.
    Figures use 2025/26 UK allowances. A planning tool, not financial advice. Download the PDF guide

    Settings

    Personal details that drive the tax engine. Changes apply and autosave immediately.

    You
    Auto-filled from date of birth.
    Each pension: £/month and the month it starts (or started) paying. DB pensions, annuities, old workplace schemes…
    Part-time work, rental, consultancy… £/month (today's money) with a from and to date. Treated as taxable income in the plan.
    Spouse / partner
    Auto-filled from date of birth.
    Assign holdings to your spouse with the “Owner” column in the Portfolio table. Their pension pot gets its own personal allowance in the drawdown maths.
    Display & app
    Shown at the right of the menu bar — handy for telling which copy (phone, GitHub, desktop) you’re looking at.
    How every part of the Cockpit works — setup, the income tuner, tax levers, actions and backups.
    Action alerts
    The 1st Alert turns action tiles amber and drives the “due soon” banner and email summary. Both alerts become reminders in the calendar (.ics) export, plus one on the due day itself. The 2nd Alert must not exceed the 1st; set it to 0 to skip it.
    Plan horizon
    State pension: full new rate is £11,973/yr (2025/26); check each forecast at gov.uk/check-state-pension. Household drawdown starts when the first of you retires; a partner still working offsets the need with their salary (taxed, NI ignored).

    Guidance only, not financial advice — figures use 2025/26 rules. Notes are saved into your scenario when you export the JSON.
